Parent requirement: [REQ-001 — Establish the workspace contract](../../req.md)

Review state: Completed. Task and implementation briefs approved; the three canonical templates delivered under `.worktree/templates/`, verified, reviewed, and accepted by the human.

## Purpose

Give humans and agents predictable starting points for requirements, task briefs, and implementation briefs.

## Scope

Specify the required metadata and content sections for `req.md`, `task.md`, and `implementation.md`. Store the templates under `.worktree/templates/` and keep them coherent with the workspace structure and linking conventions covered by [TASK-001](../TASK-001/task.md).

Explicitly distinguish mandatory sections from optional sections. Design the task brief template to stay concise and human-friendly, and the implementation brief template to provide a predictable, agent-ready structure. Allow task-specific details within the defined structure without allowing arbitrary, inconsistent formats.

## Expected outcome

The templates capture requirement intent and outcomes, keep task briefs concise and human-readable, and provide detailed agent-ready implementation briefs. Required metadata, mandatory and optional sections, and how to include task-specific details are clear; their exact definitions remain for this task's approved implementation brief.
